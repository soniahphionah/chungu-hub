Why Chungu Hub

Chungu means pot. This is where Kenya's pots live.

Great pottery is made all over Kenya, from quiet studios in Karen to village roadsides in Kakamega, but it is hard to find. Chungu Hub brings it together so you can discover who is making what, and where.

Every shop has its own page with a description, what they make, how to reach them and a map, so you can call, visit or order straight from the maker.
