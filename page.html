About Chungu Hub

Bringing Kenya's potters and the people who love their work together

Our story

A home for Kenyan pottery

Chungu Hub began with a simple problem: Kenya has wonderful potters, but they are scattered across studios, workshops, craft centres and roadside markets, and there was no single place to find them.

We are building that place. Chungu Hub is a free directory where anyone can discover pottery shops and studios, learn what each one makes, and get in touch directly. The name comes from chungu, the Swahili word for a clay pot.

🏺

Our mission

Help makers be found, and buyers find makers

We want every potter in Kenya, from a village group to a city studio, to have a page where customers can see their work and reach them. And we want buyers to have an easy, trustworthy way to find handmade pottery close to them.

Chungu Hub does not take a cut. You contact the shop directly, and you deal with the maker.
